Our Mission

Connecting Donors to Save Lives

BloodBridge was founded to eliminate the critical time lag between a patient’s blood need and finding an eligible, compatible donor nearby. By pairing smart medical compatibility with real-time road routing, every emergency request finds the quickest path to safety.

Core Platform Capabilities

🧬 Smart ABO/Rh Compatibility Engine

Our automated matching verifies all safe blood donor-recipient pairings. Universal donors (O-) and specific compatible matches are prioritized to protect patient safety.

🗺️ OSRM Road Distance Routing

Instead of straight-line distance, BloodBridge computes real driving distance and estimated travel time to ensure donors can reach the hospital quickly.

⏱️ 56-Day Safety Interval Tracking

To safeguard donor health, the platform automatically monitors donation dates, enforcing standard medical rest periods before notifying donors for future requests.

🔒 Verified Donor Network

Every donor profile goes through administrator verification to ensure accurate records, reliable contacts, and maximum emergency readiness.

Platform Roles & Ecosystem

🏥

Patients

Submit urgent blood requests with required units and partner hospital location. Monitor compatible donor responses in real-time until requirements are fulfilled.

🩸

Donors

Receive real-time match alerts for compatible emergencies nearby. Accept or decline requests, navigate to hospitals, and track donation history safely.

🛡️

Administrators

Verify donor credentials and medical eligibility, oversee hospital registries, monitor active requests across cities, and safeguard community standards.

How BloodBridge Works

  1. Request Creation: A patient or hospital initiates a blood request specifying blood group, units, hospital, and urgency level.
  2. Algorithmic Matching: The system matches eligible, verified donors based on ABO/Rh compatibility and 56-day rest rules, ranked by road driving distance.
  3. Instant Notification: Matched donors receive immediate in-app alerts detailing hospital location and required units.
  4. Response & Fulfillment: Donors accept requests, coordinate with the hospital, and complete donations, securely updating request status.
